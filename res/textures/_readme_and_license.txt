The Starry Night, Vincent van Gogh, June 1889
Oil on canvas, 73.7 x 92.1 cm. Museum of Modern Art, New York.
--------------------------------------------------------------

The painting
------------

Van Gogh died in 1890, so the painting itself is out of copyright
everywhere.

The photograph
--------------

From the Google Art Project. The original on Commons is 44,567 x 35,291
pixels and 664 MB.

File page:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Van_Gogh_-_Starry_Night_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg

Wikimedia Commons tags it PD-Art: a faithful photographic reproduction
of a two-dimensional public domain work, identified there as free of
known restrictions under copyright law, including all related and
neighbouring rights.

That doctrine is settled in the United States, and Article 14 of the
2019 EU copyright directive says reproductions of public domain visual
works are not protected unless the reproduction is itself an original
creation. Commons' own guidance lists where it is and is not settled:

  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reuse_of_PD-Art_photographs

Recorded here rather than summarised as "public domain", because the
painting and the photograph of it are two different questions and only
the first one has a simple answer.

Note that several other Starry Night files on Commons are photographs of
the canvas hanging in the museum rather than flat reproductions. Those
are new works by the photographer who took them and are not PD-Art, so
they are worse rather than better. This one is a flat scan.

What was done to it
-------------------

Downloaded 2026-09-28 as the 1920 x 1520 rendering Commons serves.

  cropped 1520 x 1520 from x = 200, keeping the whole cypress on the
    left and the moon on the right
  scaled to 1024 x 1024, lanczos

The painting is 5:4 and the board's tiles are square, so a square crop
was needed. It costs 200 pixels from each side, against the third of the
height a square crop of the Mona Lisa would have cost.
